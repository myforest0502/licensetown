# LicenseTown current free-validation policy v0.1

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: owner-approved operating policy

## Current position

LicenseTown is **not charging users now**.

The product is still in validation. There may be defects, confusing behavior, educational weaknesses, missing safeguards, performance problems, or other issues that the current builders have not yet discovered. Until enough real-use evidence exists and those risks are better understood, LicenseTown should not present itself as a finished paid service.

This is an ethical and product-quality boundary, not a pricing delay caused by missing payment plumbing.

## Current user-facing model

- normal LicenseTown use remains free during the validation phase;
- paid access enforcement remains OFF;
- live Stripe charging remains OFF;
- the existence of sandbox payment infrastructure does not authorize real charging;
- the future monthly candidate price is **¥980/month**, but it is not a current public offer;
- no user should lose learning functionality because they have not paid while this free-validation policy is active.

## Development support / 応援

If a person independently wants to support continued development, LicenseTown may describe that separately as **optional development support / 応援**.

That support must not be represented as:

- a required usage fee;
- a subscription required to keep using LicenseTown;
- preferential educational treatment;
- a promise of exam success or product completion;
- a disguised paid plan.

The intended message is: LicenseTown is currently being improved through real use. Use is not being sold yet. If someone nevertheless wants to support continued development, that support is voluntary and separate from learning access.

Any mechanism that actually accepts money for support must still receive separate owner approval before activation and must use truthful legal/payment wording.

## Future paid candidate

If and only if validation later supports charging, the current candidate is one simple plan at **¥980/month**.

The future paid contract should be re-reviewed at that time against:

- real external learner feedback;
- defect/reliability history;
- retention and repeated-use evidence;
- parent/supporter usefulness;
- actual operating cost;
- willingness to pay;
- final legal/payment wording.

The ¥980 value is therefore a future candidate, not a commitment to start charging on a particular date.

## Promotion gate

Do not move from free validation to paid service merely because the code supports Stripe.

A paid launch requires a new explicit owner go/no-go after the current state is reviewed. At minimum, confirm:

1. supported learner routes are reliable under broader real use;
2. material known defects have acceptable resolution or disclosure;
3. the product value is understandable without overstating evidence;
4. the future free/paid boundary is still appropriate;
5. current fees/costs are disclosed to the owner;
6. public HP / terms / checkout / cancellation / refund wording agree;
7. the owner explicitly approves accepting real money.

Until all of that happens, the correct state is **free validation + optional support only if separately approved**.
